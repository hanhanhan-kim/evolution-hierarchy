Grid bounds span setting means, not sampling CIs. JSON separately stores Monte Carlo SE and replicate percentiles. Undefined strength correlations stay null; valid replicate counts accompany all means.
Floors use one FW-R and one CE8 reference; transferring them to other coverage, species or within-side subnetworks is an assumption. FW-R/HB uses the requested common-quality floor, not separate calibrated FlyWire and hemibrain error models.
Excess is a signed descriptive subtraction, not a calibrated biological distance. The three metrics have different scales. Do not interpret negative excess as negative biological variation.
A common per-system floor cancels from category differences, at every grid setting. Worm between-animal here means within-CE/within-PP pairs; between-species is compared with those within-species pairs.
